Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Tessellon Games develops and operates mobile applications, including Agent_Slime and Crystalloop. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our games.

1. Data Collection and Use

We respect your privacy. Our games do not require you to create an account, register personal credentials, or provide identifying information to play. We do not collect, harvest, or store any personal data.

2. Data Handling and Storage

Because we do not gather user data, no information is shared with third parties, advertisers, or external tracking services. Any game settings and level progression are stored locally on your device.

3. Children's Privacy

We do not collect personal information from users of any age, including children. Our games operate entirely offline without requiring user registration, ensuring full compliance with international child privacy and data protection guidelines.
